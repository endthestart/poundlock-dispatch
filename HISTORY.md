# History

Poundlock Dispatch was developed in a private repository from September 2026 by
Michael Anderson. This public repository starts at 0.3.0 with a single commit of
that work; private test captures and project material were left out and replaced
with synthetic fixtures. Development continues here.

The client was first called Lockkeep. On its first start, Dispatch still copies a
Lockkeep data folder it finds into its own, keeps job keys in the old namespace,
and accepts `LOCKKEEP_*` launch variables, for installations that predate the name.
